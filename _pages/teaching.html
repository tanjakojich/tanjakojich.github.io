{% include icon.html name="graduation" %} Student Supervision

Thesis & Project Mentoring

I supervise Bachelor's and Master's theses at the Quality and Usability Lab, mainly on user experience in extended reality, multimodal interaction, and user-centred evaluation.

Focus Areas

  • User experience in VR, AR and MR
  • Multimodal interaction (hand tracking, voice, passthrough)
  • Quality of Experience and user factors
  • Serious games and exergaming
  • Data visualization in VR

Project Types

  • Bachelor's theses
  • Master's theses
  • Study and interdisciplinary media projects
  • Research collaborations
